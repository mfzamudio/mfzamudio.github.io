📝 Data Analytics: Tackling the Data

Steps for Descriptive Analytics

Descriptive Analytics is the foundation of data-driven decision making. It focuses on understanding what happened in the past by organizing and summarizing historical data. Below are the 10 essential steps:

  1. Collecting Data: Gather raw information from databases, APIs, surveys, or logs.
  2. Cleaning Data: Remove duplicates, correct errors, and handle missing values.
  3. Transforming & Aggregation: Standardize formats and consolidate data for consistency.
  4. Filtering & Reducing Noise: Eliminate irrelevant information and outliers.
  5. Segmentation & Clustering: Group data into meaningful categories for deeper insights.
  6. Visualization & Trending: Use charts and dashboards to reveal patterns and trends.
  7. Comparing: Analyze performance across time, categories, or segments.
  8. Reporting: Summarize findings in structured, easy-to-digest formats.
  9. Patterns & Insights: Identify relationships, anomalies, and recurring behaviors.
  10. Sharing & Publishing: Distribute results via dashboards, documents, or presentations to stakeholders.
