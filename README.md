## 👨‍💼 About Me

I am an electronic engineer with 23 years of experience in the telecommunications industry, specializing in solution architecture, configuration, testing, and support for major carriers. Over the last decade, I transitioned into analytics and technical leadership roles, focusing on provisioning, charging control, and complex problem-solving for large-scale deployments.

I completed a **Master's in Data Analytics** at the University of Niagara Falls (GPA 4.0, President's Distinction List every term). My training has deepened my expertise in **Python, Power BI, Tableau, SPSS, SQL, and Streamlit**, as well as statistical modelling, machine learning, data warehousing, and visualization. I actively integrate AI tools — including **Claude**, **Claude Code**, **Gemini**, and **Comet** — to accelerate development, refine technical pipelines, and apply prompt engineering across the full analytics workflow.
